Our Traditional Preparation Standards

How we prepare every jar, flour batch, and oil tin following Vedic guidelines.

🏺

Cultured Curd Bilona Ghee

Fresh cow and buffalo milk is first cultured into whole curd, churned slowly with wooden bilonas, and gently boiled on low flame to yield natural golden granules (*danedar*).

🪵

Lakdi Ghani Wood-Pressed Oil

Native yellow mustard seeds are cold-extracted below 40°C in traditional wooden kolhus, preserving natural pungent aroma and essential fatty acids without chemical solvents.

🌾

Slow Stone Chakki Ground Atta

Sehore Sharbati wheat, bajra, and juar are ground slowly on natural stone mills without heating, ensuring dietary fiber, wheat germ, and sweet natural taste remain untouched.

🥣

Naturally Sun-Dried & Unpolished

Our Desi Kala Chana and Toor Dal are dried naturally under the sun and kept 100% unpolished, without artificial water washes or synthetic polish coatings.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our organic products, shipping, and ordering.

How can I be certain your products are genuinely organic?
We partner directly with traditional farmers who cultivate crops without synthetic chemical fertilizers, pesticides, or GMO seeds. Our small-batch produce undergoes regular quality checks to ensure zero adulteration, authentic natural flavor, and purity.
What is the difference between regular market ghee and your A2 Bilona Ghee?
Most commercial supermarket ghee is boiled rapidly from leftover cream separated using high-heat industrial equipment. Our A2 Ghee is handcrafted following the traditional 5-step Bilona method: fresh A2 cow milk is cultured into curd, churned with a wooden bi-directional bilona, and the extracted makkhan is gently simmered over slow heat into golden, aromatic danedar ghee.
What are your delivery charges and shipping times across India?
We provide FREE doorstep delivery on all orders above ₹799. For smaller orders under ₹799, a nominal delivery charge of ₹60 applies. Express deliveries to metros arrive in 2–3 business days; other locations arrive in 3–5 days. Glass jars are packed in shock-absorbing eco-cushioning.
What payment methods do you accept?
You can pay securely via Direct UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), choose Cash on Delivery (COD), or complete your order seamlessly via WhatsApp.
Why are your dals and grains unpolished?
Commercial dals are frequently treated with artificial water washes and petroleum oils to give them an artificial shine, which strips the wholesome natural fiber and nutrients. Our Desi Kala Chana and Toor Dal remain 100% unpolished and sun-dried, preserving their natural taste, wholesome fiber, and plant proteins.
Can I place bulk or custom grocery orders for my family?
Yes! If you need 10kg+ bags of wheat, millets, or bulk ghee for festive occasions or monthly pantry supply, simply message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 95113 55866 for direct-from-farm pricing and custom packing.
